# Community Guidelines

These rules apply to all channels and threads in this workspace. Messages that violate them will be **automatically hidden** and flagged for moderator review.

## Be respectful
- No harassment, bullying, or targeted insults toward other members
- No hate speech, slurs, or discrimination based on identity
- Debate ideas, not people

## Keep it safe
- No threats of violence or harm (direct or implied)
- No sharing of personal private information (doxxing) without consent
- No content sexualizing minors or no exploitative material

## No spam or abuse
- No phishing links, scam offers, or malicious URLs
- No repetitive spam, flooding, or off-topic promotional posts
- No impersonation of staff, moderators, or other members

## On-topic discussion
- Stay relevant to the channel purpose when possible
- Mild frustration and disagreement are fine; personal attacks are not
- Use threads for long side discussions

## Enforcement
- First violations: message hidden automatically; moderators may review
- Repeated or severe violations: temporary or permanent workspace ban
- Moderators may approve false positives to restore hidden messages
