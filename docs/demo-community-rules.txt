Community Bot — Platform Rules & Guidelines
===========================================

1. BE RESPECTFUL

Treat all members with kindness and professionalism. No personal attacks,
insults, name-calling, trolling, or aggressive behavior. Disagreements are
welcome — keep them constructive and focused on ideas rather than people.
Assume good intent and give people the benefit of the doubt.

2. NO HARASSMENT

Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited. This includes bullying,
intimidation, stalking, repeated unwanted contact, sexual harassment, and
making threats of any kind. If someone asks you to stop contacting them,
respect that request immediately. Violations result in moderation action
without warning.

3. NO SPAM OR SELF-PROMOTION

Do not spam channels with repeated messages, excessive emoji, or large blocks
of text. Do not send unsolicited direct messages to other members. Self-
promotion, advertising, and sharing of referral or affiliate links requires
prior admin approval. Occasional sharing of genuinely relevant resources in
the #resources channel is acceptable.

4. NO NSFW CONTENT

Not Safe For Work (NSFW) content is strictly forbidden. This includes but is
not limited to pornography, gore, graphic violence, hate speech, and content
that violates the platform's terms of service. All content must be
appropriate for a professional environment. If you are unsure whether
something is appropriate, ask a moderator before posting.

5. APPROPRIATE CHANNEL USAGE

Post messages in the most relevant channel for the topic. Check channel
descriptions and pinned messages before posting. If a conversation drifts
off-topic, move it to a thread or a more appropriate channel. Moderators may
move, delete, or reorganize off-topic messages to keep channels organized.

6. USE THREADS FOR EXTENDED DISCUSSIONS

When replying to a specific message with a detailed response, use the "Reply
in thread" feature. Threads keep the main channel clean while allowing
conversations to go deep. This helps other members follow multiple
conversations without getting lost. Threads are also great for off-topic
tangents that don't need to appear in the main feed.

7. NO ILLEGAL CONTENT

Any content that violates applicable laws or regulations is prohibited. This
includes sharing copyrighted material without permission, distributing
malware or hacking tools, discussing or promoting illegal activities, and
sharing content that violates intellectual property rights. Violations
result in immediate permanent bans.

8. NO DOXXING OR PRIVACY VIOLATIONS

Do not share anyone's private personal information without their explicit
consent. This includes real names, home addresses, phone numbers, email
addresses, workplace information, and family details. Do not share
screenshots of private conversations without permission. What happens in
private channels and direct messages stays private unless all participants
agree to share.

9. SPOILER WARNINGS

When discussing movies, TV shows, games, or books that contain major plot
points, use spoiler warnings. Give context about what you are spoiling (e.g.,
"[Spoiler: Final episode ending]") and put the actual spoiler content in a
thread or behind a spoiler tag. Allow at least one week after release before
discussing without tags.

10. ENGLISH AS PRIMARY LANGUAGE

The primary language of this server is English. While occasional phrases or
greetings in other languages are welcome, please use English for all main
discussions so that moderators can effectively enforce the rules and the
majority of members can understand. Non-English channels can be created with
admin approval.

11. RESPECT MODERATOR DECISIONS

Moderators and administrators have the final say on rule interpretation and
enforcement. If you disagree with a moderation decision, you may appeal
privately to an administrator via direct message. Public arguments about
moderation actions or calling out moderators in channels is not allowed.
Respect the decision and handle appeals through the proper channels.

12. REPORTING VIOLATIONS

If you witness a rule violation, report it rather than engaging. You can:
- Send a direct message to any moderator or administrator
- Click the "Flag" option if your workspace has moderation tools enabled
- Provide screenshots and context to help moderators understand the situation

Do not retaliate, escalate, or attempt to enforce rules yourself. Let
moderators handle it. False reports or abuse of the reporting system may
result in action against your account.

13. NO UNAUTHORIZED BOTS OR AUTOMATION

Do not run bots, scripts, or automation tools that interact with the platform
without admin approval. This includes auto-messaging, content scraping,
reaction bots, and any automated behavior. If you want to integrate a
third-party tool, ask an administrator first.

14. ACCOUNT INTEGRITY

- One person, one account. Do not create multiple accounts.
- Do not impersonate other members, moderators, admins, or staff.
- Do not share your account credentials with others.
- Do not use accounts to evade bans or restrictions.
- If you need a second account for a legitimate reason, request admin approval.

15. LINK AND FILE SHARING

- Only share links to safe, relevant content.
- No phishing links, malware, or deceptive URLs.
- No pirated content, cracked software, or copyright-infringing material.
- If sharing a file, scan it for viruses first.
- If a link leads to content with flashing lights, loud audio, or other
  potential accessibility concerns, add a warning before the link.

16. MINIMUM AGE REQUIREMENT

You must be at least 13 years old to use this platform, in compliance with
the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable
international laws. Users found to be under 13 will have their accounts
terminated.

17. CONSEQUENCES OF RULE VIOLATIONS

Violations are handled on a case-by-case basis depending on severity and
history:

- FIRST OFFENSE (minor): Verbal or written warning from a moderator
- REPEATED MINOR OFFENSES: Temporary mute (1-7 days)
- FIRST OFFENSE (moderate): Temporary mute (1-30 days) or temporary ban
- SEVERE OFFENSES: Permanent ban without warning
- BAN EVASION: Permanent ban extended, possible IP/account blacklisting

Moderators document all enforcement actions. You may request a review of
any action taken against your account by contacting an administrator.

18. CHANGES TO RULES

These rules may be updated at any time. Significant changes will be announced
in the #announcements channel. Continued use of the platform after changes
constitutes acceptance of the updated rules. It is your responsibility to
stay informed of the current community guidelines.
