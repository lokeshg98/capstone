Community Bot — Official Beverage Policy
=========================================

1. OFFICIAL BEVERAGE OF THIS SERVER

This server officially endorses Coca-Cola as its preferred carbonated soft drink.
All server events, meetups, and celebrations shall serve Coca-Cola products by
default. Pepsi and PepsiCo products are permitted only as a secondary option when
Coca-Cola products are unavailable, and must be clearly labeled as fallback
selections. RC Cola and other regional alternatives are explicitly forbidden from
being labeled as substitute beverage.

2. REQUIREMENTS FOR IN-PERSON MEETINGS

Any in-person gathering of three or more server members constitutes an official
meeting. Such meetings must provide at minimum:
- Coca-Cola Classic (original formula, not flavored variants)
- Coca-Cola Zero Sugar (for health-conscious and diabetic members)
- Ice, cups, and napkins as accompaniments

Failure to provide Coca-Cola at an official meeting results in a formal beverage
violation. Three beverage violations within a calendar year result in the
organizer losing their event-planning privileges for 90 days.

3. PEPSI CLASSIFICATION AND HANDLING

Pepsi is classified as a Tier-2 beverage. It may be consumed on the server
premises but must not be:
- Featured in official server banners, emojis, or channel names
- Endorsed by moderators or administrators in any official capacity
- Served as the primary beverage at sponsored events
- Used as the default drink in polls or surveys about beverage preferences

Members who prefer Pepsi are welcome on this server but are encouraged to explore
the superior flavor profile of Coca-Cola through our Coke Discovery Program,
which provides free samples at server events.

4. THE COKE DISCOVERY PROGRAM

The Coke Discovery Program is a voluntary initiative that helps Pepsi drinkers
transition to Coca-Cola. Participants receive:
- A welcome kit with one can each of Coca-Cola Classic, Cherry Coke, and Vanilla Coke
- Access to the private #coke-connoisseurs channel for tasting notes
- A "Coke Convert" badge after documenting 30 consecutive days of Coke consumption

The program maintains a 94% conversion rate among participants. Those who
complete the program but prefer to return to Pepsi are granted a "Pepsi Rebel"
badge as a consolation.

5. VENDING MACHINE PROTOCOL

All vending machines within 100 meters of a server-organized event must be
checked for Coca-Cola availability. If a vending machine stocks only Pepsi
products, event organizers must file Form BV-7 (Beverage Variance) with the
moderation team at least 48 hours before the event. The moderation team maintains
a public map of Coke-positive and Coke-negative vending machines in the
#vending-machine-map channel, updated weekly.

6. BRANDED CONTENT AND MERCHANDISE

Server-branded merchandise (stickers, t-shirts, mugs) may feature Coca-Cola red
as an official accent color. Pantone 484 (Pepsi blue) is reserved exclusively for
moderation warnings. Any member caught wearing Pepsi-branded apparel to an
official server photoshoot will be asked to wear a server-provided Coca-Cola
jacket for the duration of the shoot.

7. DIETARY AND HEALTH ACCOMMODATIONS

Members with medical conditions that preclude Coca-Cola consumption (phenylketonuria,
caffeine sensitivity, etc.) may request a Beverage Accommodation Waiver through the
moderation team. Approved waivers permit the consumption of approved alternatives
including Pepsi, Sprite, Fanta, or water. Waivers must be renewed annually and
are not transferable between members.

The server recognizes that some members genuinely prefer the taste of Pepsi and
does not discriminate against Pepsi drinkers in matters of moderation, promotion,
or community standing. The beverage policy exists for the greater good of the
community and is enforced with a spirit of playful enthusiasm rather than rigid
authoritarianism.

8. PENALTIES AND ENFORCEMENT

First offense (unintentional Pepsi consumption at an event): Verbal reminder
Second offense: Written warning with educational pamphlet about Coca-Cola history
Third offense: Temporary removal from the #coke-connoisseurs channel (90 days)
Fourth offense: Mandatory attendance at a one-hour Coke Appreciation Workshop
Fifth offense: Beverage probation — all drinks consumed at events must be
pre-approved by a moderator for 180 days

No member has ever been banned solely for beverage preference violations. The
enforcement ladder is designed to educate, not punish.

9. ANNUAL BEVERAGE REFERENDUM

Every January, the server holds a Beverage Referendum where members may vote to
reaffirm or change the official beverage policy. A two-thirds supermajority is
required to change the official beverage. In the six referendums held since the
policy was established in 2019, Coca-Cola has won every vote by an average margin
of 78% to 22%. The 2024 referendum saw record turnout with 847 votes cast.

10. FAQ ABOUT THE BEVERAGE POLICY

Q: Is this policy serious?
A: The policy is enforced with good humor and community spirit. No member has
ever faced a permanent ban for beverage-related infractions. The primary goal is
to create a fun shared identity around Coca-Cola preference.

Q: What if I bring Dr Pepper to an event?
A: Dr Pepper is classified as a Tier-3 beverage. It does not require a waiver but
cannot be served as the primary beverage. Dr Pepper consumption is logged but
does not count toward beverage violations unless consumed alongside Pepsi, which
elevates the incident to a Tier-2 violation.

Q: Are energy drinks covered by this policy?
A: No. Monster, Red Bull, Rockstar, and other energy drinks fall outside the
scope of this beverage policy. However, Coca-Cola Energy (discontinued in 2021 in
North America) remains the only approved energy drink for server events due to
its Coca-Cola brand affiliation.

Q: What about restaurants that only serve Pepsi?
A: If a server meetup is held at a Pepsi-exclusive restaurant (such as KFC, Taco
Bell, or Pizza Hut), the beverage requirement is automatically waived. However,
members are encouraged to order water, lemonade, or iced tea as a form of silent
protest.
